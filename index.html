How it works

  1. You send your book link and tell me who you want to reach.
  2. I read your book page and send you the list of reviewers and groups before I contact anyone.
  3. I reach out with a short, honest message, and a free copy of your book where it is welcome.
  4. You get a report showing who I contacted and who replied.

Questions authors ask

Do you pay for reviews?

No. Reviewers who receive a copy choose whether to review it and what to say. Amazon does not allow paid or incentivised reviews, and I do not ask for them.

Can you guarantee sales or a set number of reviews?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. I promise the outreach described in your package.

Can you get my book made into a film or TV show?

No. I don't offer that, and I would be wary of anyone who says they can.

What if you don't finish the work?

You get a refund for the part I didn't deliver.

How do I pay?

By invoice through a standard payment service, after we agree your package in writing.
